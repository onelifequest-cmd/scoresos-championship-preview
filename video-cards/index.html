01Score OS

One operating system for your whole agency.

Score OS is the all-in-one operating system for credit repair agencies. Everything a credit repair agency needs to launch, run, and scale — in one platform. Stop duct-taping ten different subscriptions together.

02Fully Automatic Disputing

Your client signs up. Score OS takes it from there.

The report pulls itself, every negative item gets flagged, and the letters are built and ready, all before you’ve touched a thing. All that’s left is one click: approve and send.

  • Credit report pulled the moment they onboard
  • Every negative item found in seconds
  • Letters filled, stacked and ready to go
  • Maxine’s “Work Next” queue picks the next client and preps their letters, with a Manual or Fully automatic switch
  • You approve and send, that’s it
03Custom Game Plan

A personal plan for every user.

Every user gets a personal plan to start and grow their business, based on their experience. Plans can also organize client goals like mortgage readiness, a new car, or personal and business funding.

  • Personal start-and-grow plan based on experience
  • Mortgage, car and funding goals organized in the plan
  • A credit strategy at onboarding, updated after every dispute round
  • Saved to the client’s file
  • Checklists your team can walk through together
04Funding OS

Every goal, tracked from ready to sent.

See every client by goal and stage, from Funding Ready through send. Score OS checks every opportunity, not just the one they chose, and clients hear “you may qualify,” never “approved.”

  • Pipeline by goal and stage
  • Submit funding applications for review from inside Score OS
  • New funding opportunities added to the system automatically
  • Users are notified when they may qualify
  • Checks every opportunity, not just their goal
